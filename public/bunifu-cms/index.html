bunifu-cms

bunifu-cms is the content management system for Bunifu Youths Kenya. It helps our team publish and update website content, program information, events, and community updates on bunifuyouths.org.

Sign in to bunifu-cms Privacy Policy

What bunifu-cms does

Bunifu Youths Kenya runs STEM, robotics, AI, and creative technology programs for young learners. bunifu-cms is the internal tool our team uses to keep the public website accurate without changing code for every update.

Authorized administrators can:

  • Sign in to the Bunifu content management dashboard
  • Create, edit, and publish website and program content
  • Keep activities, bootcamps, centers, and outreach information up to date
  • Coordinate operational updates used by the Bunifu Youths team

Access and account information

bunifu-cms is limited to approved Bunifu Youths staff and collaborators. Sign-in uses a Google account to confirm identity and open the correct workspace. Basic account details such as name and email may be used for authentication and account context inside the platform.

Account information is used only to verify authorized access and operate the CMS securely. It is not used to reach unrelated services beyond what is required for sign-in.

Who it is for

bunifu-cms is for Bunifu Youths Kenya administrators, mentors, and approved collaborators who manage digital content. It is not a public learner portal. Learners, parents, and partners continue to use bunifuyouths.org without signing into the CMS.
