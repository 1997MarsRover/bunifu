Bunifu activity platform

bunifu-cms

bunifu-cms is the central activity platform for students and schools taking part in Bunifu Youths Kenya programs. It brings activities, session information, and important updates together in one clear place.

Sign in to bunifu-cms Privacy Policy

Activities in one place

Bunifu Youths Kenya runs STEM, robotics, AI, and creative technology programs for young learners. bunifu-cms helps participants stay connected with the activities and program information that are relevant to them.

Through the platform, authorized users can:

  • Access their Bunifu activity workspace
  • Follow relevant session and program information
  • Stay informed about activities and important updates
  • Keep school and learner participation organized

Secure access

Access is provided to participating students, schools, and other authorized users. Sign-in confirms identity and opens the correct workspace. Basic account details such as name and email may be used for authentication and account context.

Account information is used only to verify authorized access and operate the platform securely. It is not used to reach unrelated services beyond what is required for sign-in.

For students and schools

bunifu-cms supports students and schools participating in Bunifu activities. General information about Bunifu programs remains available on bunifuyouths.org, while authorized users can sign in to reach their specific activity workspace.
