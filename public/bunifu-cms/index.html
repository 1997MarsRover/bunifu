bunifu-cms

bunifu-cms is the official content management system for Bunifu Youths Kenya. It helps authorized staff publish and update website content, program information, events, and community updates that appear on bunifuyouths.org.

Sign in to bunifu-cms Read Privacy Policy

Purpose of the application

Bunifu Youths Kenya runs STEM, robotics, AI, and creative technology programs for young learners. bunifu-cms is an internal admin tool that lets our team manage the public website and related content without changing code for every update.

With bunifu-cms, authorized administrators can:

  • Sign in securely to the Bunifu content management dashboard
  • Create, edit, and publish website and program content
  • Keep public information about activities, bootcamps, centers, and outreach accurate
  • Coordinate operational updates used by the Bunifu Youths team

Why bunifu-cms uses Google Sign-In

bunifu-cms uses Google Sign-In so only approved Bunifu Youths staff and collaborators can access the admin dashboard. When you sign in, the app requests basic Google account information needed to authenticate your identity and open your authorized workspace.

Google user data is requested only to:

  • Verify that the person signing in is an authorized administrator
  • Display the signed-in user’s name and email inside the CMS for account context
  • Protect administrative access to Bunifu Youths content and operations

bunifu-cms does not use Google Sign-In to access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Contacts, or other unrelated Google services unless those scopes are explicitly shown on the consent screen for a specific approved feature.

Who this app is for

bunifu-cms is for Bunifu Youths Kenya administrators, mentors, and approved collaborators who manage digital content. It is not a public learner login portal. Learners, parents, and partners continue to use bunifuyouths.org without signing into the CMS.

Privacy and data use
