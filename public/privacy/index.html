Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026
Applies to bunifu-cms and the Bunifu Youths Kenya public website at bunifuyouths.org.

1. Who we are

Bunifu Youths Kenya (“Bunifu”, “we”, “us”) is a STEAM learning community that helps children and teens build practical skills through coding, robotics, AI, 3D design, mentorship, outreach, and competitions.

bunifu-cms is our staff content management system used to manage website content and related administrative information for Bunifu Youths Kenya.

Contact: info@bunifuyouths.co.ke · +254 712 015 793 · Afralti Waiyaki Way, Nairobi, Kenya

2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you:

  • Visit bunifuyouths.org
  • Contact us through forms, email, phone, or social channels
  • Sign in to bunifu-cms using Google Sign-In

3. Google user data used by bunifu-cms

bunifu-cms uses Google OAuth / Google Sign-In so authorized staff can access the admin dashboard. Depending on the scopes shown on the Google consent screen, we may receive basic account information such as:

  • Google account email address
  • Name
  • Profile picture
  • Unique Google account identifier

We use this Google user data only to authenticate administrators, determine whether the account is authorized to use bunifu-cms, and display account context inside the application.

We do not use Google Sign-In data for advertising, data brokerage, credit decisions, or unrelated Google product access. We do not sell Google user data.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Operate and secure bunifu-cms
  • Respond to inquiries about programs, enrollment, partnerships, and volunteering
  • Provide updates about Bunifu Youths activities when you ask us to
  • Maintain accurate public website content and operational records
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against abuse or unauthorized access

5. Information we collect outside Google Sign-In

When you contact Bunifu Youths or submit forms, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and organization or school details
  • Messages about enrollment, mentorship, sponsorship, or partnerships
  • Technical data such as browser type, device information, and approximate location derived from IP address

6. Storage, sharing, and retention

Access to Google user data obtained through bunifu-cms is limited to people and systems that need it to operate the CMS securely. We retain account and authentication information only as long as needed for access control, security, and legitimate operational purposes, or longer if required by law.

We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us host, secure, or operate our systems
  • When required to comply with law, regulation, or lawful requests
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of Bunifu Youths, our users, or the public
  • With your consent, where appropriate

7. Limited use of Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in bunifu-cms, primarily secure administrator sign-in and account identification.

8. Children’s privacy

Bunifu Youths programs serve children and teens, but bunifu-cms itself is an adult staff tool. We do not knowingly allow children to create CMS administrator accounts. Program-related inquiries from parents or guardians are handled separately through our public contact channels.

9. Your choices

You may:

  • Request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need to retain
  • Revoke Google access to bunifu-cms from your Google Account permissions
  • Contact us with privacy questions at info@bunifuyouths.co.ke

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of bunifuyouths.org or bunifu-cms after an update means the revised policy applies.
